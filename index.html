⚡ ChargeMate Changelog
v8.1.0 2 Sep 2026
  • Energy mix shown in km: on transition trips that ran on more than one charge rate, the breakdown now reads in distance instead of energy share — e.g. "30 km @ ₹20 · 10 km @ ₹7" — so you can see how far you drove on expensive vs cheap power. (km is split across rates in proportion to the energy each supplied.)
v8.0.1 2 Sep 2026
  • Tidy: log rows now use one consistent text size (first line matched to the rest), and the carpool amount pill is more compact so it sits closer to the pax count.
v8.0.0 2 Sep 2026
  • Wh/km everywhere: km/kWh is fully retired across the app in favour of Wh/km (the metric your car dashboard uses — lower is better). Converted the dashboard efficiency stat, range estimate, trip planner, trip-form live preview, and all log rows. The Efficiency Trend chart now plots Wh/km too, so the line reads the same way as the number (a dip = a more efficient stretch).
v7.16.0 2 Sep 2026
  • Trip row cleanup: first line now shows start→end % with the consumed amount in brackets (e.g. "70% → 59% · (11%)"); distance moved down next to the odometer (e.g. "8,954 → 8,993 · (39 km)"). Efficiency switched from km/kWh to Wh/km (car-dashboard style, lower is better) alongside km/%. Cost/km badge sized to match the other numbers; pax/carpool spacing tightened.
v7.15.1 2 Sep 2026
  • Fix: Trip cost-per-km was inflated by charges from before trip logging began — energy from unlogged driving (e.g. early road-trip public charges) lingered in the cost queue and got wrongly attributed to later home commutes. The cost engine now reconciles against your actual battery % at every charge and trip: a measured state of charge physically caps how much energy can be left in the pool, so stale energy is trimmed to reality. Recent home-charged trips read their true ~₹/km again.
v7.15.0 2 Sep 2026
  • Cost badge is now just ₹/km: the trip badge shows plain cost-per-km (e.g. "₹2.5/km") — directly comparable across trips of any length, no colour coding to decode. Free trips still read "Free".
  • Removed the net-of-carpool line from trip rows to cut noise. Carpool income still appears as its own badge and in the Carpool log.
v7.14.0 2 Sep 2026
  • Cost badge colour = ₹/km, not total: the badge still shows what you paid, but its colour now reflects cost per km so a long trip and a short one are comparable. Green ≈ home-rate driving, red ≈ public-rate driving, neutral in between. A ₹104/32km trip now correctly reads pricier than a ₹132/59km one. Free trips stay cyan. Thresholds are anchored to your own home/public rates and efficiency, so they mean something from the first trip.
v7.13.3 2 Sep 2026
  • Docs: Lite-edition changelog wording fix (v7.12.0 carpool badge description). No change to the full edition; version bumped to keep both in sync.
v7.13.2 2 Sep 2026
  • Fix: OCR trip consistency check no longer warns when battery % rises during a trip — that’s a legitimate regen trip (already supported elsewhere in the app), not an error. Only a genuinely backwards odometer or a crossed swap still warns.
v7.13.1 2 Sep 2026
  • Fix: OCR trip import now checks for a mismatched swap before saving — if the odometer and battery % swaps were done independently and end up crossed (or the odometer would go backwards, or battery % would rise during a trip), you get a warning before confirming instead of a silently broken record.
v7.13.0 2 Sep 2026
  • OCR import swap buttons: The screenshot-import review screen now groups fields by metric (Odometer, Battery %) instead of Start/End, with a ⇄ swap button on each row — one tap fixes a misread start/end without retyping.
v7.12.0 2 Sep 2026
  • Cost badge redesign v2: badge colour now shows what the money is, not how much — Free (cyan), pure expense (default text), covered by carpool (green), partially offset (amber). Fixes v7.11.0’s percentile colouring, which could show green for both an expensive trip and the carpool income badge. Net-of-carpool line simplified to two states: covered / still paying.
v7.11.0 2 Sep 2026
  • Cost badge redesign: Trip cost ranked against your own history — cheapest 25% green, middle 35% muted, upper 25% amber, top 15% red. No trip is ever invisible. Each badge gains a subtle tinted background for easier scanning.
v7.10.0 1 Sep 2026
  • Cost colour coding: Trip cost badges coloured by magnitude (green=cheap, white=average, yellow=pricey, red=expensive). Energy mix rates coloured by source (green=home, blue=free, yellow/red=public). Net-of-carpool: green=covered, yellow=you paid significantly.
v7.9.0 1 Sep 2026
  • Cost per trip: Every trip now shows its true ₹ cost, computed with FIFO energy accounting — each charge is a priced "lot" of kWh (home/office/public) and each trip draws from the oldest energy first. A trip that ran partly on home and partly on public power is priced accordingly, not at a flat rate.
  • Trips show the energy mix when it spans more than one rate (e.g. "80% @ ₹7 · 20% @ ₹18"), so you can see exactly where an expensive leg came from.
  • Net after carpool: carpool trips show cost minus what riders paid (e.g. "₹272 − ₹266 = net ₹6"), and read "covered" when the riders more than paid for the trip.
  • Energy charged before you started logging is priced at your lifetime average ₹/kWh, so early trips still get a sensible cost.
